Research

Many systems that shape our daily lives can be viewed as networks, including communication systems, nervous systems, transportation, infrastructure, and disease-spread networks. These networks are not fixed: they grow, change, and respond to the activities of the people or agents within them. My research aims to understand how their structure affects reliability, connectivity, the spread of information or disease, and collective behaviour. This work seeks to provide useful ideas for designing better, safer, and more resilient systems.

Research Interests

Probability theory, random graphs, percolation, interacting particle systems.
